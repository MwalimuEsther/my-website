About Esther

My teaching journey

I am Esther Ndirangu, a passionate educator, Kiswahili enthusiast, career coach, and digital content creator with over 15 years of teaching experience. I use my knowledge and creativity to inspire learners, empower teachers, and make learning meaningful and enjoyable.

Why I make these videos

I create educational, inspirational, and entertaining videos to promote the beauty of Kiswahili, share life lessons, and make knowledge accessible to everyone. My goal is to educate, inspire, and transform lives, one video at a time.
